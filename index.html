About

I am an undergraduate student from Vietnam, majoring in Talented Program - Computer Science at the School of Information and Communication Technology (SOICT), Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).

I am conducting research at the Modeling, Simulation and Optimization Laboratory (MSOLab), under the supervision of Professor Huynh Thi Thanh Binh.

My research interests are not limited to any specific domain — I am always exploring new problems, challenges, and creative ideas. At the moment, I am focusing on: Combinatorial Optimization, Machine Learning Theory, Multi-Agent Learning, and Computer Vision.

Prospects

I am preparing for Ph.D. applications in the United States while strengthening my publication record and academic profile. My dream programs include MIT, Stanford, Princeton, Harvard, UC Berkeley, and CMU, though I still view them as aspirational goals.

I am also seeking Ph.D. scholarship opportunities at NUS, NTU, MBZUAI, Tsinghua University, and Peking University, though these are not the only paths I am considering. In addition, I am open to visiting student and research assistant opportunities in labs aligned with my research interests. If there may be another suitable opportunity, I would be grateful for the chance to discuss it via tuankiet.nv2501@gmail.com.
